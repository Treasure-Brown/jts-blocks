# JT's Blocks

A local recreation of the Yahoo! Games puzzle **JT's Blocks**. Open `index.html` in a browser — no install, no build.

## How to play

- Click a group of **two or more** same-colored blocks that touch on their sides, then click again to clear them.
- Bigger groups score more (`5 × n²` points).
- **Mixers** (the pale tiles with a shuffle mark) scramble a 5×5 area around them. Click once to preview, again to mix. The mixer itself turns into the most common nearby color.
- Blocks fall down and columns pack left.
- When no moves remain, you need the **score to beat** to continue. Clearing most of the board adds a bonus.

## Rules source

Board size, mixer behavior, scoring, bonuses, and level targets follow Brian Kobashikawa's Yahoo Widget / HTML recreation of the original game.
